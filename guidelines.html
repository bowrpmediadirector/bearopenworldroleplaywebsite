Community Standards

Community Guidelines

Follow these rules to keep roleplay immersive, fair, and respectful for everyone.

1) Respect Everyone

No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.

2) Stay In Character

Use realistic roleplay and avoid fail RP behavior.

3) No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches, or unfair advantages.

4) Follow Department SOPs

All department members must follow standards and training.

5) Staff Decisions

Staff rulings are final during active sessions.
